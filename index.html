00 — Notice

Every move you make on-chain is *watched.* Every buy, every sell, every transfer is read, labeled and filed under your wallet. The chain never forgets. So we *h1de* it.

01 — Trace

How one wallet
becomes a person.

  1. Funded from one accountThe first SOL came from somewhere with a name on it.
  2. Same hours, every nightActivity lands in the same four hours. Habits are fingerprints.
  3. Same counterpartiesIt keeps paying the same wallets. Wallets that pay each other get grouped.
  4. One labelEnough links and the label sticks: one person, every wallet.
  5. H1ddenCut the link and the file goes blank.

        06 — Agents

        Agents move money too.

        • POST/api/scan

          The exposure file of any wallet, streamed step by step. Nothing about the wallet is stored.

        • POST/api/route

          Quote a private route between two wallets through NEAR Intents. Dry quotes move nothing.

        • GET/api/stats

          Moves h1dden, SOL routed, wallets scanned. Counts only.

        • POST/api/mcp

          An MCP server with three tools: scan_wallet, route_quote and h1de_stats.

        • GET/agent.txt

          Plain-text instructions for any agent that lands here.

        07 — Limits
        
    
        What it h1des.
        What it can't.
        
    
        
      
        H1des
          
        
        • The on-chain link between your wallet and your ghost.
          • 
        
        • Which wallet made a buy, a sell, a swap or a send.
          • 
        
        • Your main wallet's history from anyone watching the ghost.
          • 
        
        • Who you are from us: no account, no email, no list of addresses.
          • 
      
        
      
        Can't h1de
          
        
        • The route network (NEAR Intents and its solvers) sees both ends of a route.
          • 
        
        • Equal amounts at close times can be matched. Split them and wait.
          • 
        
        • Sending from a ghost straight to your main wallet links them again.
          • 
        
        • Your IP is seen by the RPC and by this site. Use a VPN if that matters.
          • 
        
        • Ghost keys live in this browser only. Clear it and they are gone, so back them up.
          • 
      
        
    
        
    
        
      
        What it costs
        Routes cost the solver network about 0.1%. Swaps go through Jupiter at its normal fees. H1DE takes nothing from your moves.
        
      
        Who pays for it
        Creator fees from $H1DE pay for the RPC, the watcher and this site. Nothing here pays holders or promises a price.
        
      
        What it is
        H1DE is independent experimental software. It is not affiliated with NEAR, Jupiter or any wallet. Use small amounts first.
        
    
        
    
        
  
        

  
        
    
        08 — FAQ
        
    
        Asked in private.
        
    
        
      
        
        
        What is H1DE?
        Ghost wallets and private routes for Solana. Your wallet pays a one-time NEAR Intents deposit address, a solver pays your ghost from its own wallet, and every move after that comes from a wallet with no on-chain link to you.
        
        
        Is it a mixer?
        No pool and no custody. Each route is a cross-wallet swap through NEAR Intents: a solver fills it from its own funds. If a route fails, the SOL goes back to the wallet that paid it.
        
        
        Do you hold my funds or keys?
        No. Ghost keys are made and kept in your browser. Your main wallet signs its own payments. H1DE builds transactions and passes them on; it never sees a key.
        
        
        What if I clear my browser?
        The ghosts are gone with it, and so is anything they hold. Press Back up keys and keep the file somewhere safe before you fund a ghost.
        
      
        
      
        
        
        What does the scan read?
        Only public chain data: the wallet's signatures, balance, token count, the transaction that first funded it and its last 25 transactions. H1DE stores none of it. The file is written fresh each time.
        
        
        Which wallets work?
        Phantom, Solflare, Backpack and any wallet that speaks Wallet Standard. Your wallet only ever pays a route's deposit address.
        
        
        Is this legal?
        Privacy is normal. Breaking the law is not. Don't use H1DE to hide stolen funds or dodge sanctions. H1DE is independent experimental software.
        
        
        Is there a token?
        $H1DE. The contract address appears on this site at launch. Anyone posting one before that is not us.
